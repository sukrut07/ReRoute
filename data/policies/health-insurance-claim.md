# Health Insurance Claim — Synthetic Policy

## Required evidence
- Policy identification
- Identity verification
- Hospital documentation
- Discharge summary
- Claim details

## Verification
Critical identity fields should be consistent across submitted evidence.

## Conflict handling
If critical identity information conflicts, do not continue automatically. Ask for clarification or route to human review.

## AI boundaries
AI may assist with extraction, classification, verification, explanation and journey routing. AI must not independently approve or reject a claim.

This policy is synthetic and exists only for the Reroute hackathon demonstration.
